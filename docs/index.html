ascendy ♡

An NFC- and QR-based focus app for Android. Tap a physical anchor to lock distracting apps and websites. Tap it again to unlock.

Get it

  • Google Play
  • Latest APK (GitHub Releases)

Features

  • NFC & QR anchors — tap a tag or scan a code to start and stop sessions. The physical commitment is the point.
  • App blocking — pick any installed apps to block during a session. Accessibility service detects and redirects immediately.
  • Website blocking — local DNS sinkhole blocks domains by name, even in browsers that use DNS-over-HTTPS. Runs entirely on-device.
  • Strict mode — opt-in per list. No manual end — only the anchor or the safety timer gets you out.
  • Allow-list mode — invert the list: only the apps you allow are accessible during a session.
  • Safety timer — every session auto-ends after a configurable max (default 4 hours). Can't be bypassed.
  • Emergency override — one-use escape hatch with a friction-tax: type a sentence verbatim to confirm you mean it.
  • Lockdown mode — opt-in. Activates device-admin to block uninstalling mid-session, and seals the accessibility/app-info settings pages so the blocker can't be switched off. Safety timer remains the guaranteed exit.
  • Scheduled sessions — set recurring focus blocks by time of day. Re-arms automatically.
  • Pomodoro timer — start a timed session from the app without needing a physical anchor.
  • Streaks & stats — daily focus time, weekly chart, streak counter. Mascot accessories unlock at 7, 30, and 100 days.
  • Three themes — Kawaii (pink), Tough (iron), Neutral (slate). Every string is per-theme.
  • Quick Settings tile — toggle a manual session from the notification shade.
  • Home screen widget — shows session status and streak at a glance.
  • Multiple blocklists — create named lists and assign them to specific anchors.
